Your dispatch line

{{carrier}} runs dispatch through Backroute. Your loads, check-ins and paperwork come by text and in the app. Text or call the dispatch line any time: {{dispatch}}. It answers in your language.

Your first day

  1. Open the link in the text from dispatch, or go to {{site}}/login. Sign in with your phone number (you get a code by text).
  2. Tap I agree to get texts and calls from dispatch.
  3. Turn on notifications in Profile. On an iPhone, add the app to your home screen first.
  4. Before your first load, do the pre-trip inspection in the app.

On a load

  • At each stop, swipe when you arrive, then tap I'm loaded or I'm unloaded.
  • Take a photo of the BOL at pickup and the signed POD at delivery.
  • No time for the app? Just text: loaded, unloaded, running 1 hr late, or send the photo.
  • Broke down? Text or call. Dispatch finds a shop or a tow and tells the broker.

Good to know

  • Crash or injury: call 911 first, then dispatch.
  • Reply STOP to stop texts, HELP for help. You can switch to app notifications instead of texts in Profile.
  • Questions about pay or home time? Ask on the same line. Anything only {{carrier}} can decide goes to them.
