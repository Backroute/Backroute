Your first weeks with Backroute

Backroute is your dispatcher. It reads broker emails, works the rate, sends your drivers the load, keeps up with them on the road, and gets the paperwork and the invoice out. You decide how much it does on its own, and you can stop it any time.

What it does for you

  • Reads the broker emails sent to your Backroute address and answers each one.
  • Asks for a rate at or above your lowest rate per mile, never under it.
  • Sends the driver the load, and checks in before each pickup and delivery.
  • Gets the BOL and the signed POD from the driver (a photo in the app or by text).
  • Sends the invoice with the POD, claims detention, and follows up on late payments.
  • Texts and calls drivers in their own language.
  • Asks you whenever a decision is yours. It shows up in Needs you on Home.

We move up one step at a time, together, and only when you agree with what it's doing.

Day one (about 30 minutes, on a call with us)

  • Sign in with your phone number and check your trucks and drivers.
  • Set your lowest rate per mile, billing email and factoring (Settings).
  • Upload your W-9 and certificate of insurance (Settings → General → Your papers).
  • Name who signs rate cons (Settings → Your rules).
  • Turn on phone alerts (Settings → Notifications). On an iPhone, add Backroute to your home screen first.
  • Forward broker load emails to your Backroute address, or CC it on threads.
  • Forward or upload a few months of old rate cons (Settings → Bring your history).
  • Your drivers get a text with the app link. Ask them to sign in and tap I agree.
