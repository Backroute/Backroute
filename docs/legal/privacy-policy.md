# Privacy Policy (DRAFT for attorney review)

**Who we are.** [Backroute legal entity], [address], [privacy contact email].

**What we collect, and why.**

| Data | From | Used to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, phone, email, role | You, or your carrier's owner | Sign you in; reach you about loads |
| Company details: MC, DOT, address, insurance, W-9, bank or factoring details you upload | The carrier | Setup packets, invoices, broker websites |
| Loads, rates, brokers, documents (rate cons, BOLs, PODs) | The carrier, brokers' emails, uploads | Dispatching and billing |

| Photos and documents from the driver app | Drivers | Proof of delivery, claims |
| Logins to other websites (encrypted) | The carrier, or accounts we open for it | Carrier setup and scheduling on those sites |
| Usage and device data, push notification tokens | Your browser | Security, reliability, notifications |

**Who we share it with.** Brokers, shippers, receivers and partners the carrier works with, as needed for the work;
service providers that run the service (hosting and database, AI model provider, texting and calling, email, payments,
maps and routing, ELD providers the carrier connects); and when required by law. We don't sell personal information.

**AI processing.** Messages and documents are processed by an AI model provider under terms that don't allow it to
train on them. [Confirm with the provider's current terms.]

**How long we keep it.** While the carrier's account is open, and [30] days after, except records we must keep by law
(for example invoices).

**Your choices.** Reply STOP to texts to stop them; ask your carrier's owner or us to correct or delete your
information; [state-specific rights per counsel: California (CCPA/CPRA) and others].

**Security.** Access rules in the database limit each person to their own carrier's data (and drivers to their own
truck). Website passwords are encrypted with a key only our servers hold. [Per counsel: breach notice commitments.]
