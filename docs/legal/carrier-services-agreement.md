# Carrier Services Agreement (DRAFT for attorney review)

Between [Backroute legal entity], a [State] [entity type] ("Backroute"), and the motor carrier named at sign-up
("Carrier"). Effective when the Carrier's owner accepts it in the app.

## 1. What Backroute does

1.1 Backroute provides software, including an artificial-intelligence dispatcher (the "AI"), that performs dispatch
services for the Carrier: finding loads, communicating and negotiating with freight brokers and shippers, booking loads
within the rules the Carrier sets, communicating with the Carrier's drivers, scheduling appointments, preparing and
sending invoices, detention, layover and other claims, carrier setup packets, and related paperwork.

1.2 Backroute is a dispatch service acting for the Carrier. Backroute is not a freight broker, freight forwarder or
motor carrier, does not arrange transportation for shippers, and is not a party to any load, rate confirmation or
broker-carrier agreement. The Carrier is the motor carrier on every load and keeps all of a carrier's
responsibilities.

1.3 A small support team ("Support") handles what the AI hands off: safety emergencies, work on other companies'
websites the AI couldn't finish, system failures, and anything the Carrier asks a person to do.

## 2. The Carrier's authority to Backroute

2.1 The Carrier appoints Backroute as its limited agent to do, on the Carrier's behalf and in its name:

- (a) communicate with brokers, shippers, receivers, repair shops, factoring companies and the Carrier's drivers by
  email, text and phone, including calls placed and answered by the AI;
- (b) request, negotiate and accept loads, counter-offers, accessorial charges and changes, **only within the settings
  the Carrier chooses in the app** (including its lowest rate per mile and its autopilot setting);
- (c) sign rate confirmations and similar load documents electronically, in the name and title of the person the
  Carrier names in the app as authorized to sign (the "Authorized Signer"), when the document matches the terms agreed
  for that load;
- (d) complete carrier setup, onboarding and dock-scheduling forms on third-party websites, including opening accounts
  and signing in with credentials the Carrier provides or Backroute creates for the Carrier, and submitting them as set
  out in the app; and
- (e) send invoices, claims and the Carrier's documents (W-9, insurance certificate, operating authority, notice of
  assignment and others the Carrier uploads) to the parties they're meant for.

2.2 The Carrier confirms that the Authorized Signer has authority to bind the Carrier, and that it will keep the
Authorized Signer, its settings and its documents current in the app.

2.3 The Carrier may limit or withdraw any of this authority at any time in the app (for example by choosing "Ask me
first", turning off "Let the AI do these itself" for websites, or removing the Authorized Signer), effective for actions
not yet taken.

## 3. The Carrier's responsibilities

3.1 The Carrier is solely responsible for its operating authority, insurance, safety, compliance with FMCSA and state
rules (including hours of service, drug and alcohol testing and vehicle maintenance), its drivers, the equipment, and
the loads it hauls, including cargo loss and damage.

3.2 The Carrier will: review what the app asks it to decide; keep its rules and documents accurate; get each driver's
consent to texts and calls before adding them (see the driver consent); and tell Backroute at once of any error it
notices.

3.3 The Carrier decides whether to accept any load. Backroute's suggestions and the AI's actions within the Carrier's
settings are not a guarantee that a load is suitable, legal or profitable.

## 4. Fees and billing

4.1 The Carrier pays [$ amount] per truck per month for each truck in its Backroute account[, plus [__]% of the line-haul
rate of loads booked through Backroute]. The first [14] days are free. Fees are billed monthly in advance by card through
Stripe; the truck count follows the trucks in the account and changes are prorated.

4.2 If a payment fails, the Carrier has [7] days to update its card. After that the AI stops booking new loads until
payment is made; loads already booked continue to be supported.

4.3 Backroute may receive referral fees from partners the Carrier chooses to use (for example factoring or insurance).
Backroute will disclose these in the app.

## 5. Data, credentials and confidentiality

5.1 The Carrier owns its data. Backroute uses it to provide the services and to improve them, and doesn't sell it.
Backroute may use de-identified, aggregated data (for example how long docks keep trucks) to improve the service for
all carriers.

5.2 Passwords to third-party websites are stored encrypted, used only on those websites to perform the services, and are
not visible to Backroute personnel through the app.

5.3 Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential.

## 6. Accuracy and limits

6.1 The AI can make mistakes. Backroute designs the service so the AI acts only within the Carrier's settings and asks
the Carrier or Support when it isn't sure, but does not guarantee any outcome.

6.2 EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS". [Warranty disclaimer language per counsel.]

6.3 Backroute's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees the Carrier paid in the [3] months before the
claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, lost profits, or cargo loss or damage.
[Carve-outs per counsel: gross negligence, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches.]

6.4 The Carrier will indemnify Backroute against claims arising from the Carrier's operations, drivers, equipment,
cargo, and compliance with law.

## 7. Term and ending

7.1 Month to month. Either party may end it with notice in the app or by email; it ends at the end of the paid month.
7.2 On ending, the Carrier can export its data from the app for [30] days. Backroute then deletes it, except what it must
keep by law.
