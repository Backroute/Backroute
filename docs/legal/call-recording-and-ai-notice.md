# What the AI says on calls, and recording (DRAFT for attorney review)

**Every call, in the first sentence**, the AI says it's an AI and who it's calling for. From the app today:

- Calling a broker: "Hi, this is the AI dispatcher for [Carrier], MC [number]. This call is transcribed. I'm calling on
  your [lane] load…"
- A broker calling back: "[Carrier] dispatch, this is the AI dispatcher. This call is transcribed…"
- Calling a driver: "Hi [name], this is the AI dispatcher for [Carrier], checking on your load."
- Answering the dispatch line: "Hi, it's your AI dispatcher for [Carrier]. What do you need?"

**If someone says they won't deal with an AI**, the AI thanks them, ends the call, and follows up by email only; it
doesn't call that broker again.

**Recording.** [Decide with counsel.] Today the app keeps **transcripts** of what was said (needed to do the work) and
does **not** keep audio recordings. Broker calls say "This call is transcribed"; driver calls and the dispatch line don't
yet. Confirm whether transcription needs the same notice as recording in all-party-consent states. If audio is recorded, add to the opening line: "This call may be recorded."
In all-party-consent states that sentence must come before anything is recorded, and the call must not be recorded if
the other side objects.

**Texts** from the AI are signed with the carrier's name and say they're from its dispatch line (see the driver
consent).
